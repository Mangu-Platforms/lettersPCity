Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is calendar synchronization?
Letters City uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 authentication and end-to-end encryption to keep your calendar data secure. We never store your calendar service passwords, and all data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections.
Can I sync multiple calendars from the same service?
Yes! You can connect and sync multiple calendars from Google, Outlook, and iCloud. Each calendar can be individually managed and color-coded for easy identification.
What happens if I disconnect a calendar?
When you disconnect a calendar, syncing will stop immediately. Events already synced to Letters City will remain visible in read-only mode. You can reconnect at any time to resume full synchronization.
How often do calendars sync?
With real-time sync enabled, changes are pushed instantly. If real-time sync is disabled, calendars automatically sync every 15 minutes. You can also manually trigger a sync at any time.
Can I create events from Letters City?
Absolutely! With two-way sync enabled, you can create, edit, and delete events directly in Letters City, and all changes will be reflected in your connected calendar services.
Does calendar integration work on mobile devices?
Yes! Calendar integration is fully supported on our mobile apps for both iOS and Android. All features work seamlessly across desktop and mobile platforms.
