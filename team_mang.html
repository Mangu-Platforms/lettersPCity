Frequently Asked Questions

How many domains can I add?

With our Signature and Enterprise plans, you can add unlimited custom domains. The Essentials plan supports one custom domain.

Do I need to transfer my domain?

No, you don't need to transfer your domain. We'll guide you through the simple DNS configuration process.

How long does setup take?

Typically, setup takes less than 15 minutes. DNS propagation may take up to 24-48 hours to complete globally.

Can I use subdomains?

Yes, you can create email addresses using subdomains of your verified domain.

Is there additional cost?

There's no additional Letters City fee for custom domains, but you'll need to maintain your domain registration with your registrar.

Can I remove a domain?

Yes, you can remove domains at any time from your dashboard. All associated email addresses will be deactivated.
