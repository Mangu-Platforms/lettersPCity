Popular Questions

How can I recover a deleted email?

Deleted emails go to the Trash folder for 30 days. You can restore them from there unless permanently deleted.

What if I forgot my account password?

Use the "Forgot Password" option on login. You’ll receive a secure reset link via backup email or phone.

Can I schedule emails for later?

Yes. When composing, click the arrow next to Send and select "Schedule Send" to pick a future time.

Is my email visible to staff or admins?

No. All content is end-to-end encrypted. Not even Letters City staff can read your messages.

Can I use my custom domain?

Yes. On Signature or Enterprise plans, you can map custom domains via the DNS settings in your account dashboard.
